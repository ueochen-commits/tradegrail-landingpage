# TradeGrail Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-29

## Product Overview
TradeGrail is a web trading journal and post-trade review tool. Users record trades, screenshots, costs, reasons and review notes, then inspect account/strategy performance. This is software for recording and reflection; it does not execute orders or promise returns. Public site: https://www.tradegrail.net. Authenticated app: https://dashboard.tradegrail.net. Early beta; start with free access. Paid plan availability and final prices must be checked in the app; do not invent prices or mature commercial guarantees.

## Target Audience
Primary working hypothesis: Chinese-speaking active traders who want a repeatable review process, especially GC/NQ/ES futures users reflected in current customer feedback. Secondary: English-speaking traders with the same manual/CSV journaling need. These are priorities to test, not researched market-size claims.

## Jobs and Problems
Capture why a trade happened, retain before/after screenshots, understand net results after fees, and review execution separately from one trade's outcome. Excel/Notion/paper are legitimate alternatives; do not portray competitors as incapable without evidence. Current feedback concerns onboarding, fee/P&L clarity, simultaneous screenshots/charts and reliable typing. Do not publish private messages or customer screenshots as testimonials without permission.

## Differentiation and Scope
Verified in dashboard source/release records: manual entry with custom broker or none; CSV import with explicit P&L basis; free tier supports two screenshots per trade; linked review notes; account/symbol fee templates; opt-in GC/NQ/ES standard-contract calculations; theme support; risk references are reminders and do not block journaling. Embedded CME/COMEX market data may be unavailable under widget licensing; never promise free live futures data. Do not generalize support to micro contracts or all brokers. API integrations must be described individually after validation.

## Objections and Switching
Users can start manually without binding a broker; imports need correct column mapping and net/gross basis. They can try one real record before migrating a full history. Concern about time, accuracy and setup is addressed with a complete first-trade guide, explicit fee examples, and honest limitations. No claims of audited security, certifications, customer profit improvement or integration scale without evidence.

## Competitive Landscape
Excel/Notion are adjacent approaches. TradeZella, TraderSync and TradesViz are discovery candidates, not reviewed comparisons. Publish a comparison only after checking current official capabilities, dates and material differences.

## Customer Language / Voice
Use “交易日志”, “交易复盘”, “手续费”, “净盈亏”, “入场理由”, “第一笔交易”. Plain, specific and calm. Avoid “稳赚”, guaranteed alpha/returns, invented user numbers, fake reviews, unsupported superlatives and daily market commentary used as search filler.

## Proof Points
Evidence: source code and dated release/acceptance records. No independently verified customer-count, country-count, return improvement, paid-conversion or security-certification statistics are available. Interface illustrations are examples, not user-performance evidence. Authorship can be “TradeGrail product team”; do not invent named experts or credentials.

## Goals and Measurement
Business goal: qualified acquisition and activation, followed by retained use and paid conversion when available. Funnel: organic/AI referral → useful landing/guide → signup CTA → actual signup → personal account → first real trade → review/return use. A signup click is not a completed signup. Traffic, Search Console access and analytics baseline are unknown; never report them as zero. Do not send emails, trade content or account identifiers to analytics.

## Learnings
No outcome experiments yet. SEO/GEO changes are hypotheses until measured with consistent cohorts and denominators.
