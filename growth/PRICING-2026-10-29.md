# TradeGrail launch pricing draft — 2026-10-29

Owner requirements: display one payment currency based on network IP country, use the Chinese payment channel for mainland China and Creem USD elsewhere, affordable pricing with controlled costs, aim for tens of thousands of dollars monthly, and do not publish personal identity. Public-policy publication and merchant review remain paused at the owner's request. This is a tested local proposal, not an announcement that live payments are ready.

## New purchase prices

| Market | Pro monthly / yearly | Elite monthly / yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mainland China IP (CN) | CNY 29 / 290 | CNY 69 / 690 |
| Other IP countries | USD 9 / 90 | USD 19 / 190 |

Annual price equals ten monthly payments, about a 16.7% discount. Annual charges are collected in one payment. Domestic plans are prepaid without automatic debits; Creem subscriptions automatically renew until canceled. Taxes and final totals appear at checkout. Seven-day refund request policy remains in the unpublished draft. New lifetime offers are removed because their ongoing AI/storage costs have no recurring revenue; existing purchased rights are retained.

Payment country is independent of language. VPN exit country changes the network country. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan codes use the USD market; only CN maps to CNY. No IP, precise location or country profile is stored for pricing. Both repos share identical versioned price/geo definitions. Initial HTML displays neutral paid-price placeholders, avoiding a flash of the wrong currency. If the market request fails, show a visible retry rather than inventing a price. The payment-creation endpoint independently checks the current market; webhooks, payment-status reconciliation and access to an existing subscription's billing portal are never geographically blocked.

The existing production domains are behind Cloudflare. Accept its visitor-country header only when the Vercel-controlled forwarding IP belongs to Cloudflare's official published proxy ranges; otherwise use Vercel's country header. Do not trust arbitrary CF-IPCountry headers. If a verified proxy supplies no country header, fail visibly until its geolocation setting is fixed. Cloudflare geolocation setting, actual forwarded headers and mainland/overseas production traffic still require deployment acceptance; a local country fixture does not prove production geolocation. No Trusted Proxy Enterprise upgrade, DNS changes or security bypass was made.

## Cost controls in the draft

| Plan | AI requests per UTC calendar month | Daily ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 30 | 5 |
| Pro | 300 | 30 |
| Elite | 600 | 60 |

No rollover; annual plans also receive calendar-month quotas. Limits are enforced atomically across coach, analysis, reports and CSV AI mapping. Ordinary manual trade entry is unaffected. Existing active paid subscriptions retain their original AI rights until those entitlements expire; new purchases create separate entitlement rows rather than overwriting old rows. A new SQL migration is prepared and tested in isolated PostgreSQL, not applied to production.

AI input is bounded to 12,000 UTF-8 bytes of message text and output to 800/1,000/1,800/2,000 tokens depending on feature. Oversized context is explicitly shortened while retaining the beginning and end. DeepSeek's current API account lists deepseek-flash and deepseek-v4-pro; the draft replaces legacy deepseek-chat calls with deepseek-flash, non-thinking mode. No model-quality or paid-generation acceptance is claimed from the read-only model list. Model-output acceptance remains part of the release checklist.

Published peak Flash prices checked 2026-10-29: USD 0.30 / million uncached input tokens and USD 1.20 / million output tokens. For a deliberately conservative planning scenario of 14,000 input tokens including message overhead and 2,000 output tokens, one call costs about USD 0.0066; fully consumed new allowances imply approximately USD 1.98 Pro / USD 3.96 Elite / USD 0.198 Free monthly AI cost. These are planning estimates using published prices, not observed bills, universal tokenization guarantees or a guarantee against losses. Legacy paid unlimited access, accumulated screenshot storage, free-user volume and other API endpoints remain operational costs. Monitor actual provider usage and revisit quotas rather than promising unlimited AI to new users.

## Payment contribution and scale

Creem publishes 3.9% + USD 0.40 per successful transaction. At USD 9 monthly, approximately USD 8.249 remains after this fee alone; at USD 19, USD 17.859. Annual normalized monthly receipts after this fee are approximately USD 7.174 Pro and USD 15.183 Elite. These amounts are before AI, storage, hosting, support, refunds, payout/FX costs, acquisition and income/business taxes. Do not call them profit.

At a hypothetical 70% Pro / 30% Elite mix:

- All monthly: average monthly revenue USD 12; USD 20,000 MRR needs about 1,667 active subscribers.
- All annual: recognized monthly revenue averages USD 10; USD 20,000 MRR needs 2,000 active subscribers. Annual checkout cash receipts must not all be counted as that month's recurring revenue or profit.
- A target of USD 20,000 monthly net profit needs more users and an actual cost, refund, tax and acquisition model. No trustworthy actual cost or conversion/churn baseline is available yet. Do not buy advertising or promise a profit based on these scenarios.

Before acquiring large traffic, measure confirmed signup, first real trade, activated account, paid conversion, cancellation, refund and cohort retention. A signup click is not a registered user, a test payment is not revenue, and a demo trade is not activation. Search Console and real funnel analytics remain outstanding; the existing hook is not a live analytics installation. Never send emails, journal content, trade details or P&L to audience analytics.

## Acceptance and release boundaries

Local app tests (85), public site tests (9), builds, landing TypeScript check and isolated PostgreSQL assertions pass. The dashboard retains 43 pre-existing TypeScript diagnostics, with none in the new payment/AI modules. Local native-browser checks covered Chinese UI with only USD prices and English UI with only CNY prices, monthly/annual switching and privacy-safe terms output. These are fixtures, not actual foreign/mainland visitor tests. Four new versioned Creem sandbox products (9/90/19/190 USD) and sandbox catalog amounts were verified; old products were not repriced. Existing Vercel Preview environment product IDs still refer to the earlier sandbox version and must be synchronized with the new code at the next preview deployment. Real keys are absent; real payments remain disabled.

A signed domestic callback previously marked the order paid before membership upsert and relied on an absent unique user constraint. The draft uses a service-only atomic RPC, preserves old memberships, extends already-paid time for same-plan renewal, handles duplicate callbacks and rejects payment amount / transaction reuse. Its schema and server must ship together; do not deploy either half alone. Existing old-price pending domestic invoices remain reconcilable. Test in a payment sandbox before enabling live sales.

Still required before launch: resume owner-paused policy publication and merchant review; verify Cloudflare country forwarding with actual traffic; apply/record the new policy migration as part of the billing release; synchronize versioned sandbox IDs into Preview; verify successful payment, webhook, returned status, duplicate notification, cancel/refund and model generation; complete Creem approval/live credentials and owner-required financial/identity actions; recheck both public domains. Keep the live SEO health-check baseline at 16 until the 22-page release; rehearse with EXPECTED_PUBLIC_PAGES=22 and update the published baseline at launch. Do not equate sandbox catalog verification with successful payment or live acceptance.
