# TradeGrail launch pricing — 2026-10-29

Owner requirements: display one payment currency based on network IP country, use the Chinese payment channel for mainland China and Creem USD elsewhere, affordable pricing with controlled costs, aim for tens of thousands of dollars monthly, and do not publish personal identity. The owner approved these prices and resumed release/review. Brand-only legal and pricing pages are public; real USD collection remains disabled pending final acceptance and merchant approval.

## New purchase prices

| Market | Pro monthly / yearly | Elite monthly / yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mainland China IP (CN) | CNY 29 / 290 | CNY 69 / 690 |
| Other IP countries | USD 9 / 90 | USD 19 / 190 |

Annual price equals ten monthly payments, about a 16.7% discount. Annual charges are collected in one payment. Domestic plans are prepaid without automatic debits; Creem subscriptions automatically renew until canceled. Taxes and final totals appear at checkout. The published policy allows a refund request within seven days. New lifetime offers are removed because their ongoing AI/storage costs have no recurring revenue; existing purchased rights are retained.

Payment country is independent of language. VPN exit country changes the network country. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan codes use the USD market; only CN maps to CNY. No IP, precise location or country profile is stored for pricing. Both repos share identical versioned price/geo definitions. Initial HTML displays neutral paid-price placeholders, avoiding a flash of the wrong currency. If the market request fails, show a visible retry rather than inventing a price. The payment-creation endpoint independently checks the current market; webhooks, payment-status reconciliation and access to an existing subscription's billing portal are never geographically blocked.

The existing production domains are behind Cloudflare. Accept its visitor-country header only when the Vercel-controlled forwarding IP belongs to Cloudflare's official published proxy ranges; otherwise use Vercel's country header. Do not trust arbitrary CF-IPCountry headers. If a verified proxy supplies no country header, fail visibly until its geolocation setting is fixed. Public www/apex requests returned SG/USD with countrySource=cloudflare, verifying actual CDN country forwarding. Mainland behavior is covered by local fixtures; an actual mainland network check remains unperformed. No Trusted Proxy Enterprise upgrade, DNS changes or security bypass was made.

## Cost controls

| Plan | AI requests per UTC calendar month | Daily ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 30 | 5 |
| Pro | 300 | 30 |
| Elite | 600 | 60 |

No rollover; annual plans also receive calendar-month quotas. Limits are enforced atomically across coach, analysis, reports and CSV AI mapping. Ordinary manual trade entry is unaffected. Existing active paid subscriptions retain their original AI rights until those entitlements expire; new purchases create separate entitlement rows rather than overwriting old rows. The launch policy migration20261029040343 is applied; isolated PostgreSQL and rollback-only production-schema assertions passed.

AI input is bounded to 12,000 UTF-8 bytes of message text and output to 800/1,000/1,800/2,000 tokens depending on feature. Oversized context is explicitly shortened while retaining the beginning and end. DeepSeek's current API account lists deepseek-flash and deepseek-v4-pro; the release replaces legacy deepseek-chat calls with deepseek-flash, non-thinking mode. Four authenticated AI endpoints returned200 using synthetic inputs; the fifth daily request succeeded and the sixth was rejected with429. This functional acceptance is not a general model-quality guarantee.

Published peak Flash prices checked 2026-10-29: USD 0.30 / million uncached input tokens and USD 1.20 / million output tokens. For a deliberately conservative planning scenario of 14,000 input tokens including message overhead and 2,000 output tokens, one call costs about USD 0.0066; fully consumed new allowances imply approximately USD 1.98 Pro / USD 3.96 Elite / USD 0.198 Free monthly AI cost. These are planning estimates using published prices, not observed bills, universal tokenization guarantees or a guarantee against losses. Legacy paid unlimited access, accumulated screenshot storage, free-user volume and other API endpoints remain operational costs. Monitor actual provider usage and revisit quotas rather than promising unlimited AI to new users.

## Payment contribution and scale

Creem publishes 3.9% + USD 0.40 per successful transaction. At USD 9 monthly, approximately USD 8.249 remains after this fee alone; at USD 19, USD 17.859. Annual normalized monthly receipts after this fee are approximately USD 7.174 Pro and USD 15.183 Elite. These amounts are before AI, storage, hosting, support, refunds, payout/FX costs, acquisition and income/business taxes. Do not call them profit.

At a hypothetical 70% Pro / 30% Elite mix:

- All monthly: average monthly revenue USD 12; USD 20,000 MRR needs about 1,667 active subscribers.
- All annual: recognized monthly revenue averages USD 10; USD 20,000 MRR needs 2,000 active subscribers. Annual checkout cash receipts must not all be counted as that month's recurring revenue or profit.
- A target of USD 20,000 monthly net profit needs more users and an actual cost, refund, tax and acquisition model. No trustworthy actual cost or conversion/churn baseline is available yet. Do not buy advertising or promise a profit based on these scenarios.

Before acquiring large traffic, measure confirmed signup, first real trade, activated account, paid conversion, cancellation, refund and cohort retention. A signup click is not a registered user, a test payment is not revenue, and a demo trade is not activation. Search Console and real funnel analytics remain outstanding; the existing hook is not a live analytics installation. Never send emails, journal content, trade details or P&L to audience analytics.
